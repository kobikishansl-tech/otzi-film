# DYATLOV PASS — treatment

Red paper-cut documentary, 396 s. Logline: *Nine hikers cut from one sheet of red paper; the mountain cut them loose; sixty years later, science explains the cut.*

## The medium IS the story

- **One folded strip, one cut → a chain of nine joined figures** (fold–cut–unfold native move). The chain opens the film and returns at the end, standing on the ridge — with a gap where the tenth man stood.
- **The tent cut from the inside** is the film's signature shot: the jagged cut opens *toward* the camera, cold light spills through the holes (holes are light, inverted — cold, not warm).
- **Scraps have a future:** the paper scraps of the cold open become the falling snow of the whole film; the wind-deposited slab is drawn as a thickening pale paper layer.
- **Everything is one flat sheet:** the ridge's sawtooth edge stands up into the slope; the case file in Act IV is the same paper as the map in Act I.

## Palette (ground family ≠ hero family)

- **Night world (default):** indigo papers — sky `#0f142a` (ink tile), far slope `#1f2a55`, near slope `#29356e`, snow surfaces pale blue-grey `#7a88ba` / `#8fa0cc`, treeline `#28346c`.
- **Heroes:** hikers deep red `#b8191b` (near limbs `#c4162c`, far `#8a0c20`), the tent red-orange `#d2201f` — the only large red piece at night.
- **Day world (S3, S4 start, S14):** rice paper `#f2e8d0`, far hills pale `#e0877f`, mid `#cf4f4a`, ink-drawn map lines `#3a2a1c`.
- **Gold foil:** once — the low sun of the coda. **Case-file paper:** rice, stamps in red ink.

## Avoid

- No LemoLab credit, no demo characters (girl, Nian), no rosette/window-flower props, no Chinese New Year imagery — the paper-cut grammar only.
- Red-on-red: hikers are the only red on indigo grounds; on rice-paper day grounds they pop by contrast.
- No dissolve transitions: sheets slide/peel/turn only.
- Respect: no gore, no monster imagery; theories shown as paper pieces being cut away, not dramatized.
